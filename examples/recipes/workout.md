# `workout` crate — driving energy for training

```
High-energy electronic and hip-hop for strength training and intervals.
Tempo 125-145 bpm with strong percussive drive. Mix of techno, drum &
bass, big-beat, and the occasional rap track with hard production.
Examples: The Prodigy, Justice, Modeselektor, Run the Jewels, Death Grips,
Daft Punk, Pendulum. Avoid ambient, downtempo, anything below 120 bpm,
and anything sentimental.
```
