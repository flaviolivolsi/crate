# `casual` crate — music you just like

For non-focus listening: dinner, coding-with-vocals-fine, shower, commute.

```
Eclectic mix of indie rock, alt-pop, soul, neo-soul, and adjacent
genres. Mid-tempo (90-120 bpm), good melodies, lyrical depth welcome.
Bias toward less-mainstream artists but tracks should be high quality.
Examples: Tame Impala, Vulfpeck, Khruangbin, Snail Mail, Black Pumas,
Phoebe Bridgers, Mac DeMarco. Avoid stadium rock, country, and anything
overproduced.
```

Refine over time. The LLM remembers what's already in the library each run.
